The UASE Journey: Vision, Milestones & Growth.

UASE Tech Studio began with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between complex technological challenges and accessible, impactful solutions. Simultaneously, we aimed to nurture digital talent from the grassroots up.

What started as a humble initiative by Usty has rapidly evolved into a multi-dimensional tech brand. Today, we touch lives through professional training programs, cutting-edge web services, and continuous innovation.

Our scope has broadened significantly over the years. We've transitioned from offering essential virtual assistance and technical support to developing full-fledged, scalable applications that solve real-world problems. UASE Tech Studio stands as a beacon for digital empowerment and youth transformation through technology. We don't just teach or build; we create immersive experiences, solve tangible problems for businesses, and guide individuals from tech curiosity to unwavering tech confidence.

Our growth is marked by an expanding technological stack and a deepening understanding of diverse industry needs. We excel at translating abstract ideas into tangible, high-performing digital products that truly stand out. We are proud of our commitment to excellence and innovation and constantly look forward to future possibilities, striving to be Africa’s leading grassroots technology hub.

2014: Career Foundations

Began learning coding from various sources. Concurrently, joined Learnavers, Tigervers, and Totems, transitioning into the dynamic world of remote virtual assistance, IT administration, IT support, and digital freelancing, building a reputation for efficiency and reliability.

Joined Edge Meter Company Limited as a Technical Assistant, gaining invaluable hands-on experience with real-world electrical and ICT systems, further solidifying practical problem-solving skills. Also served as Executive Assistant at T4U Engineering Company Limited.

Began working on foundational software projects, including calculators, counters, timers, countdowns, and static websites. Continued executive and IT assistant roles with Sunstart Integrated Services Limited and AY Global Integrated Consult Limited, collaborating with Edge Meter Company Limited.

2025: Official Platform Launch & Strategic Growth

Officially launching the UASE website (this platform!) and setting UASE Tech Studio officially live. Focusing on building more real-time, impactful projects and further expanding training initiatives.
